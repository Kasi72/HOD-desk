soft, exact, calm.

A tactile porcelain workspace for a busy head of department. Raised surfaces are things you can press; inset wells hold things you type or read. One rule above all: you can always tell what the source said, what the AI read into it, and what you approved.

07Do and don’t

Do Put the source quote next to every AI suggestion. One teal action per screen. Status as a word plus colour. Targets 44 px or more.
Don’t Make an AI guess look solid or approved. Put light text on raised porcelain. Stack more than two depth levels. Show clinical text outside the vault.
